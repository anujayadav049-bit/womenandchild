High Risk Pregnancy Care

Specialized Care for Complicated Pregnancies | Sector-31, Gurgaon

A pregnancy is called high-risk when mother or baby has increased risk of complications. At Women and Child Care, we provide advanced monitoring, early detection and 24x7 emergency support for diabetes, hypertension, twins, IVF and advanced maternal age pregnancies.

Who Needs High Risk Care?

Age >35, Diabetes, BP, Thyroid, Twin pregnancy, Previous miscarriage / C-section, IVF pregnancy, Low lying placenta.

Advanced Scans

Doppler, Growth Scan, NST

Lab Monitoring

Sugar, BP, Thyroid, Urine

24x7 Emergency

ICU, NICU Backup Ready

Our High Risk Protocol:
Gestational Diabetes Management
Preeclampsia / High BP Control
Twin / Multiple Pregnancy
Previous LSCS / VBAC Counseling
IVF / IUI Pregnancy Care
Preterm Labour Prevention
